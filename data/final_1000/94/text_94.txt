Ben Cura
Ben Cura( born 30 September 1988) is a British- Argentine actor and director of film, television and theatre.

René Clément
René Clément( 18 March 1913 – 17 March 1996) was a French film director and screenwriter.

Henri Decoin
Henri Decoin( 18 March 1890 – 4 July 1969) was a French film director and screenwriter, who directed more than 50 films between 1933 and 1964. He was also a swimmer who won the national title in 1911 and held the national record in the 500 m freestyle. He competed in the 400 m freestyle at the 1908 Summer Olympics and in the water polo tournament at the 1912 Summer Olympics.

Brian Johnson (special effects artist)
Brian Johnson( born 1939 or 1940) is a British designer and director of film and television special effects.

Marc Frédérix
Marc Frédérix( 1919–2004) was a French art director. He was nominated for an Academy Award in the category Best Art Direction for the film" Is Paris Burning?"

Is Paris Burning? (film)
Is Paris Burning? is a 1966 French- American epic historical war film directed by René Clément, starring an ensemble cast, about the liberation of Paris in August 1944 by the French Resistance and the Free French Forces during World War II. The script was based on the book of the same title by Larry Collins and Dominique Lapierre.

Peter Levin
Peter Levin is an American director of film, television and theatre.

Dortoir des grandes
Dortoir des grandes, is a French crime drama film from 1954, directed by Henri Decoin, written by François Chalais, starring Jean Marais and Louis de Funès. The film is also known under the titles:" Girls' Dormitory" and" Inside a Girls' Dormitory"( USA).

Ian Barry (director)
