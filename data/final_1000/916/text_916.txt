Searching for Monica
Buscando a Mónica( Searching for Monica) is a 1962 Argentine- Spanish black- and- white film musical film drama directed by José María Forqué. The film premiered on 29 January 1962 in Madrid and was named" El Secreto de Mónica". It was first shown in Argentina on 29 March in Buenos Aires. It starred Alberto de Mendoza and Carmen Sevilla.
