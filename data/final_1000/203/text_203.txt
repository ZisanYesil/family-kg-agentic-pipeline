Make-Up (1937 film)
Make- Up is a 1937 British drama film directed by Alfred Zeisler and starring Nils Asther, June Clyde and Judy Kelly. It was a circus film made by an independent production company at Shepperton Studios.

Maid of Salem
Maid of Salem is a 1937 film made by Paramount Pictures, directed by Frank Lloyd, and starring Claudette Colbert and Fred MacMurray.

Annapolis Salute
Annapolis Salute is a 1937 film. A few scenes were shot at William Paca House.

Gadis jang Terdjoeal
Gadis jang Terdjoeal is a 1937 film from the Dutch East Indies( now Indonesia). It was directed by The Teng Chun, his first film to recognise native interests.

The Perfect Specimen
The Perfect Specimen is a 1937 film directed by Michael Curtiz and starring Errol Flynn and Joan Blondell. The picture is based on a novel by Samuel Hopkins Adams.

Outlaws of the Orient
Outlaws of the Orient is a 1937 film directed by Ernest B. Schoedsack.

Men in Exile
Men in Exile is a 1937 film directed by John Farrow. A" B" movie from Warner Bros, it was the first feature Farrow directed.

Trouble in Morocco
Trouble in Morocco is a 1937 film directed by Ernest B. Schoedsack and starring Jack Holt.

Lover's Prayer
Lover's Prayer is a 2001 American- British drama film written and directed by Reverge Anselmo and starring Kirsten Dunst and Nick Stahl. It is based on Ivan Turgenev's" First Love" and Anton Chekhov's" The Peasant Women".
