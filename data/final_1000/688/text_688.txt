Sex Is Zero
Sex Is Zero is a 2002 South Korean film written and directed by Yoon Je-kyoon, starring Im Chang- jung and Ha Ji-won. In the style of American gross- out comedies like" American Pie", it follows the exploits of a group of college students, which eventually takes a serious turn. " Sex Is Zero" sold 4,089,900 tickets in South Korea, making it the fifth most popular film of 2002. A sequel," Sex Is Zero 2", was released in December 2007, starring most of the original cast.
