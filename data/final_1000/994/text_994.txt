Dry Summer
Dry Summer( a.k.a." Reflections") is a 1964 black- and- white Turkish drama film, co-produced, co-written and directed by Metin Erksan based on a novel by Necati Cumalı, featuring Erol Taş as a tobacco farmer, who dams a river to irrigate his own property and ruin his competitors. It is also available in an English dubbed U.S. theatrical release titled" Reflections" produced by William Shelton and edited by David E. Durston. Martin Scorsese has supported the film's preservation and it is available on DVD.
