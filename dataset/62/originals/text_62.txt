Erkko Kivikoski: Erkko Kivikoski (2 July 1936 – 11 August 2005) was a Finnish film director and screenwriter. He directed nine films between 1962 and 1981. His 1963 film "Kesällä kello 5" was entered into the 14th Berlin International Film Festival. His 1981 film "Night by the Seashore" was entered into the 12th Moscow International Film Festival where it won a Special Diploma.

Glamour Boy (film): Glamour Boy is a 1941 American comedy film directed by Ralph Murphy and written by Val Burton, F. Hugh Herbert and Bradford Ropes. The film stars Jackie Cooper, Susanna Foster, Walter Abel, Darryl Hickman, Ann Gillis, William Demarest and Jackie Searl. The film was released on December 5, 1941, by Paramount Pictures.
